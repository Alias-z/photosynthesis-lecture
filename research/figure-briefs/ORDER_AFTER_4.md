## Overlaps found in the current slides

1. **4b and 12** both teach absorption and the S₂ → S₁ heat drop. In 12 it is point 1 and half of the figure; in the fates animation, beats 1–2.
2. **5, 6 and the pigments animation** all teach Chl a vs Chl b. It is point 2 on slide 6.
3. **7 and 9:**
   - 7's points (membrane location, "they work at the same time", P680/P700 naming) do not match its title.
   - "Both photosystems absorb at once" is on both slides.
4. **12 and 14** both teach "fluorescence reports the competition" (12 point 2, 14 lead).
5. **ETR order:** the route overview (9) comes after the water details (8). The lecturer asked for the overview first.

## Variant 2: one slide fewer

12 and 13 merge into one **"Too much light"** slide: S₁'s exits, then how the leaf adds heat dissipation (NPQ), then repair. This frees about 2 minutes for the animations.

## Rejected: teach all four fates at 4b

Photochemistry, closed reaction centres and NPQ would be named before students know what a reaction centre or the lumen is. The 22 Oct 2026 sequence correction already moved fates after electron transport for this reason.

## Basis

- The 22 Oct 2026 sequence correction (LECTURE_OUTLINE.md): fates and fluorescence after electron transport, before photoprotection.
