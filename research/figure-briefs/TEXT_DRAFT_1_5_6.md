Theme claims, one fact per line ("/" marks a line break), short enough to leave room above the sources line.

## Slide 1 · Photosynthesis
- Lead: Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy.
- **Light reactions.** Pigments absorb light / Water is split, O₂ is released, ATP and NADPH are made
- **Calvin–Benson cycle.** ATP and NADPH fix CO₂ into sugar / The carbon in bread comes from air
- **Overall equation.** 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂, with "light" over the arrow

## Slide 5 · Why are leaves green?
- Lead: A pigment absorbs a photon only if its energy matches an electron jump in the pigment.
- **Absorption spectrum.** Chlorophyll absorbs blue and red strongly, green weakly / Leaves reflect and transmit more green: they look green
- **Green light.** Leaves still absorb 50–90 % of green / It reaches deeper cells and drives photosynthesis there
- **Accessory pigments.** Chlorophyll b and carotenoids widen the absorbed band / Red algae: phycoerythrin absorbs green

## Slide 6 · Light-harvesting complexes
- Lead: In full sun one chlorophyll absorbs only ~10 photons per second, so light-harvesting complexes (LHC) collect light for each reaction centre.
- **Energy transfer.** Antenna pigments pass on energy, not electrons / Only the reaction centre releases an electron
- **Chlorophyll a and b.** Chl b carries –CHO where Chl a carries –CH₃ / This shifts its absorption peaks
- **Two antennas.** LHCII feeds photosystem II (PSII) / LHCI feeds PSI and absorbs beyond 700 nm
