# Annotated reading list

Initial research pass checked 21 October 2026: **29 new Zotero records; 25 verified local PDFs**, in **My Library → Photosynthesis** (`LHLJBVYZ`). The later lecture additions below are local references and are outside those verified Zotero counts.

Selection excludes predatory and questionable journals. Current reviews are separated from older foundations and historical documents. Journal reputation does not make every interpretation a consensus; the final section is explicitly a research debate.

PDF status below refers to the actual Zotero attachment and file check, not merely a publisher download link. An absent saved PDF does not establish that the work is inaccessible.

## Opening reference, confirmed with its context on 22 October 2026

[Johnson (2016), Photosynthesis](https://doi.org/10.1042/EBC20160016). *Essays in Biochemistry* **60**, 255–273. Foundational overview connecting light reactions, ATP/NADPH and carbon fixation. Saved with slide 1's confirmed Context and in the handoff. Zotero status was not checked in this update.

## LHC references added for the lecture, 22 October 2026

### van Amerongen & Croce (2013)

[Light harvesting in photosystem II](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-013-9824-3). *Photosynthesis Research* **116**, 251–263. Foundational review.

For the next slide after pigments: organization of LHCII, excitation transfer between pigments and complexes, core antennae, and reaction-centre trapping. [Natural strategies for photosynthetic light harvesting](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.1555). *Nature Chemical Biology* **10**, 492–501. Foundational perspective.

For antenna function and design principles across photosynthetic organisms. Relevant figure subjects: antenna function (Figure 4), spectral tuning (Figure 6), and excitation-energy transfer (Figure 7). Publisher metadata and figure listings checked; full text was not available through the web reader in this update. Zotero status was not checked.

Supports the recorded explanation of excitation transfer, pigment coupling, parallel pathways and reaction-centre trapping times. Full text checked through the authors’ university repository. Zotero status was not checked.

## Current reviews

### 1. Johnson (2025)

[Structure, regulation and assembly of the photosynthetic electron transport chain](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-025-00847-y). *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology*. **Review**.

Overall framework for electron transport, regulation, and assembly. Use the corrected version; the publisher fixed a concentration unit.

Zotero item `MMBUEKUF` · **PDF verified**.

### 2. Liu et al. (2026)

[Structures of Photosynthetic Supramolecular Complexes](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biophys-021424-011156). *Annual Review of Biophysics*. **Review**.

Current structural overview of photosynthetic supramolecular assemblies, useful when drawing complexes and excitation pathways.

Zotero item `NFGGFHKI` · **PDF verified**.

### 3. van Amerongen & Croce (2025)

[Nonphotochemical quenching in plants: Mechanisms and mysteries](https://doi.org/10.1093/plcell/koaf240). *The Plant Cell*. **Review**.

Evidence-led discussion of qE mechanisms and uncertainties. Its proposed antenna-switch model should be labelled as a working model.

Zotero item `5KPX3YCI` · **PDF verified**.

### 4. Marulanda Valencia & Pandit (2024)

[Photosystem II Subunit S (PsbS): A Nano Regulator of Plant Photosynthesis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2023.168407). *Journal of Molecular Biology*. **Review**.

Focused account of PsbS structure, proton sensing, and unresolved interactions. Publisher text/abstract checked; no local PDF saved.

Zotero item `JK9RHNTS` · **Record saved; no PDF saved at verification**.

Date check: 2024 issue; first online in December 2023.

### 5. Glowacka (2025)

[Mechanisms underpinning natural variation in non-photochemical quenching kinetics](https://doi.org/10.1042/bst20253087). *Biochemical Society Transactions*. **Review**.

Useful for PsbS, VDE, ZEP, and NPQ kinetics. Use the mechanistic sections; crop-engineering and environmental material are outside this lecture.

Zotero item `PFMX85N2` · **PDF verified**.

### 6. Elias et al. (2024)

[Oxygenic Photosynthesis in Far-Red Light: Strategies and Mechanisms](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-physchem-090722-125847). *Annual Review of Physical Chemistry*. **Review**.

Far-red oxygenic photosynthesis across organisms. Distinguish specialized pigment systems from the far-red synergy found in ordinary crop leaves.

Zotero item `XXYR4JDT` · **PDF verified**.

### 7. Kohzuma et al. (2025)

[Evolution and regulatory diversification of plastid F1FO-ATP synthase](https://doi.org/10.1093/pcp/pcaf100). *Plant and Cell Physiology*. **Review**.

Plastid ATP synthase structure and regulatory diversity, including the chloroplast rotor and redox control.

Zotero item `DEFUQFU7` · **PDF verified**.

## Recent primary studies

### 8. Li et al. (2026)

[In situ structures of plant photosystem supercomplexes](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10847-3). *Nature*. **Primary research**.

Native rice chloroplast structures retain additional LHCII trimers lost in purification. A strong recent example of why membrane context matters.

Zotero item `7TU9NFS3` · **PDF verified**.

Date check: Published online 29 July 2026; September 2026 issue.

### 9. Bhowmick et al. (2023)

[Structural evidence for intermediates during O2 formation in photosystem II](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06038-z). *Nature*. **Primary research**.

Time-resolved structural evidence for intermediates during oxygen formation; connects the Kok cycle with modern experimental snapshots.

Zotero item `QDH7HIT8` · **PDF verified**.

### 10. Degen et al. (2024)

[Proton Gradient Regulation 5 is required to avoid photosynthetic oscillations during light transitions](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erad428). *Journal of Experimental Botany*. **Primary research**.

Tests PGR5-dependent regulation and oscillations during light transitions. Useful evidence for energy balancing without asserting a settled molecular FQR identity.

Zotero item `LKN5WND3` · **PDF verified**.

Date check: 2024 issue; first online 28 October 2023.

## Methodological and conceptual foundations

### 11. Murchie & Lawson (2013)

[Chlorophyll fluorescence analysis: a guide to good practice and understanding some new applications](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/ert208). *Journal of Experimental Botany*. **Methods review**.

Practical basis for distinguishing dark-adapted maximum PSII efficiency, operating efficiency, estimated electron transport, and carbon assimilation.

Zotero item `2JU3SP7R` · **PDF verified**.

### 12. Zhen & Bugbee (2020)

[Far‐red photons have equivalent efficiency to traditional photosynthetic photons: Implications for redefining photosynthetically active radiation](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.13730). *Plant, Cell &amp; Environment*. **Primary research**.

Far-red efficiency measured with shorter-wavelength illumination. Read the illumination context before repeating the title as a general statement.

Zotero item `MS34UD7Q` · **PDF verified**.

### 13. Terashima et al. (2009)

[Green Light Drives Leaf Photosynthesis More Efficiently than Red Light in Strong White Light: Revisiting the Enigmatic Question of Why Leaves are Green](https://doi.org/10.1093/pcp/pcp034). *Plant and Cell Physiology*. **Primary research**.

Green-light penetration and photosynthesis under strong white light; supports a more accurate explanation of green leaves.

Zotero item `QY8DD3NB` · **PDF verified**.

### 14. Hahn et al. (2018)

## Historical primary papers

### 18. Strutt (1871)

[XV. On the light from the sky, its polarization and colour](https://doi.org/10.1080/14786447108640452). *The London, Edinburgh, and Dublin Philosophical Magazine and Journal of Science*. **Historical primary research**.

The wavelength dependence of small-particle scattering; read its original particle-cloud context before telling the modern sky story.

Zotero item `ZXNYCWN5` · **PDF verified**.

Date check: Original publication 1871; a modern digital publication date is not the discovery year.

### 19. Einstein (1905)

[Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19053220607). *Annalen der Physik*. **Historical theoretical paper**.

Einstein on light quanta. Pair with Planck history and distinguish photoelectric emission from chlorophyll excitation.

Zotero item `B3XZN24S` · **PDF verified**.

Date check: Original publication 1905; the later online date is digitization metadata.

### 20. Stokes (1852)

[XXX. On the change of refrangibility of light](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstl.1852.0022). *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London*. **Historical primary research**.

Wavelength-changing emission, including quinine and leaf-green solutions; useful for the fluorescence story.

Zotero item `NTEIQ7CA` · **PDF verified**.

### 21. Emerson & Arnold (1932)

[A separation of the reactions in photosynthesis by means of intermittent light](https://doi.org/10.1085/jgp.15.4.391). *Journal of General Physiology*. **Historical primary research**.

Intermittent-light experiments that separated fast photochemistry from slower processes and helped develop the photosynthetic-unit concept.

Zotero item `7MFEBME6` · **PDF verified**.

### 22. Hill (1937)

[Oxygen Evolved by Isolated Chloroplasts](https://doi.org/10.1038/139881a0). *Nature*. **Historical primary research**.

Oxygen evolution from isolated chloroplasts using artificial acceptors; a vivid route into partial reactions and electron flow.

Zotero item `4XKI7SNR` · **PDF verified**.

### 23. Hill & Bendall (1960)

[Function of the Two Cytochrome Components in Chloroplasts: A Working Hypothesis](https://doi.org/10.1038/186136a0). *Nature*. **Historical hypothesis paper**.

A foundational working hypothesis behind the Z scheme. Do not present the modern chain as fully established by this one paper.

Zotero item `PAGLPRCG` · **PDF verified**.

### 24. Mitchell (1961)

[Coupling of Phosphorylation to Electron and Hydrogen Transfer by a Chemi-Osmotic type of Mechanism](https://doi.org/10.1038/191144a0). *Nature*. **Historical hypothesis paper**.

Chemiosmotic coupling: an electrochemical gradient as the intermediate between electron transfer and phosphorylation.

Zotero item `NU9AHWZI` · **PDF verified**.

### 25. Jagendorf & Uribe (1966)

[ATP formation caused by acid-base transition of spinach chloroplasts.](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.55.1.170). *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*. **Historical primary research**.

Acid–base transitions drive transient ATP formation in chloroplast preparations in darkness: a direct, memorable experiment for coupling.

Zotero item `UWBQVF5G` · **PDF verified**.

### 26. Noji et al. (1997)

[Direct observation of the rotation of F1-ATPase](https://doi.org/10.1038/386299a0). *Nature*. **Primary research**.

Direct observation of F1 rotation with a fluorescent actin marker. The experiment was powered by ATP hydrolysis.

Zotero item `5CZIHGW3` · **PDF verified**.

### 27. McCree (1971)

[The action spectrum, absorptance and quantum yield of photosynthesis in crop plants](https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-1571(71)90022-7). *Agricultural Meteorology*. **Primary research**.

Crop action spectra, absorptance, and quantum yield. A foundation for wavelength accounting, rather than a current review.

Zotero item `SFFJ75FJ` · **Record saved; no PDF saved at verification**.

### 28. Blackman (1905)

[Optima and Limiting Factors](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.aob.a089000). *Annals of Botany*. **Historical conceptual paper**.

The limiting-factor framework for the final light-response-curve discussion.
