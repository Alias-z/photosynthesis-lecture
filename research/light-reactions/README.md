## Journal selection

Predatory and questionable journals are excluded. The selected venues are established scholarly journals; priority goes to major reviews and relevant primary experiments. A DOI, open-access status, or a publisher's name alone is not the basis for inclusion. Contested interpretations are identified separately from established teaching principles.

For the less familiar historical sources, [Photosynthesis Research is the official journal of the International Society of Photosynthesis Research](https://link.springer.com/journal/11120/aims-and-scope). [Photosynthetica documents its editorial process and Institute of Experimental Botany, Czech Academy of Sciences affiliation](https://www.ps.ueb.cas.cz/artkey/inf-990000-0200_For-Authors.php); its Kautsky article is labelled here as a historical note/letter rather than a current mechanistic review.

## Scope

This packet follows the agreed light-reaction sequence. The light-response curve stays at the end. Environmental controls, sun/shade acclimation, and engineering applications remain outside this lecture. The story bank is a set of choices, not a suggestion to fit every story into 45 minutes. The existing slides and animations were not changed.

The search is a curated teaching research pass, not a systematic review or a claim to include every paper published by the checking date. Primary journal pages, Crossref metadata, PubMed, saved papers, and authoritative historical archives were used. Proposed animations are explicitly separated from historical evidence.
