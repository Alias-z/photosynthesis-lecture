# One chain, spotlighted (slides 7–10; lecturer's plan, 27 Oct 2026, late night): first level, for approval

## The schema (drawn once, reused on every slide and animation)
- Thylakoid membrane, horizontal: stroma above, lumen below.
- Left to right: photosystem II (with antenna) · plastoquinone · cytochrome b₆f · plastocyanin (lumen side) · photosystem I (with antenna) · ferredoxin and FNR (stroma side) · ATP synthase.
- In and out:
  - light at photosystem II and at photosystem I;
  - 2 H₂O → O₂ + H⁺ (lumen);
  - H⁺ into the lumen;
  - NADP⁺ → NADPH (stroma);
  - ADP + Pᵢ → ATP (stroma).
