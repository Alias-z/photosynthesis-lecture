Grana vs stroma lamellae (lateral heterogeneity): each piece goes where its concepts are first taught.

| Where | What goes there | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| Slide 5 | text unchanged; animation "Into the leaf" gains beat 4 "Stacked and unstacked": PSII + antenna in the grana, PSI and ATP synthase in the unstacked membranes because their large stromal parts do not fit between stacked membranes (lecturer, 27 Oct 2026) | the zoom passes through the grana anyway; the full reasons come in chunk 5 |
| Chunk 5, opening slide (the chain on the membrane) | Hook candidate: "If stacking catches more light, why isn't the whole membrane stacked?" PSII + LHCII sit in the grana; PSI and ATP synthase in the stroma lamellae, where they reach NADP⁺ and ADP and have room for their large stromal parts; b₆f in both; plastoquinone and plastocyanin carry electrons between them; the separation keeps PSI from draining PSII's excitation | The chain cannot be understood without knowing it runs across both membrane types |
| Chunk 5, routes slide | state transitions: LHCII leaves the grana for PSI; cyclic flow runs in the stroma lamellae | builds on the map |
| Chunk 6, repair | damaged PSII moves from the grana to the stroma lamellae, where a new D1 is inserted | repair is taught there |
| Chunk 6, sun vs shade | sun chloroplasts: smaller grana, more stroma lamellae (more PSI, ATP synthase, repair); shade: tall grana (slide 5) | the trade-off closes the loop with slide 5 |

History hook to verify: Andersson & Anderson 1980 (BBA) separated stacked from unstacked thylakoid fragments and found PSII in the stacks, PSI and ATP synthase outside.
