## Slide 7 Photosystem II: the whole function, essentials only
| | Proposed |
|---|---|
| Title | Photosystem II |
| Lead | Photosystem II uses light energy to split water: it releases O₂, passes the electrons to plastoquinone and leaves H⁺ in the lumen. |
| Water splitting. | 2 H₂O → O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ / The O₂ we breathe comes from water |
| Electrons to plastoquinone. | Light at the reaction centre (P680) moves each electron across the membrane / Plastoquinone takes the electrons, with H⁺ from the stroma, and carries them on in the membrane |
| Acidic lumen. | The H⁺ from water stay in the lumen / Flowing back through ATP synthase, they drive ATP synthesis |

Figures: five new variants of the whole PSII function, after Shevela et al. 2023 Fig. 1 (on the photochemistry board): water in → O₂ + H⁺ out (lumen); e⁻ → plastoquinone (stroma side); lumen H⁺ → ATP synthase. No cofactor chain.
