# Chunk 4 · Photochemistry: concepts first, then the stories (27 Oct 2026, draft for the lecturer; nothing built)

Comes in from chunk 3: the energy has reached the reaction centre ("What does the reaction centre do with it?").
Goes out to chunk 5: electrons on plastoquinone and acid in the lumen ("Where do they go, and what is the acid for?").

## Concepts students take home

| # | Concept | Take-home |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reaction centre, charge separation | The same chlorophyll a, held by the reaction-centre protein (P680), gives away an electron when excited: light energy becomes separated charge |
| 2 | Stable separation | The electron moves downhill along carriers across the membrane, so it does not come back |
| 3 | Water oxidation | P680⁺ takes electrons back from water at the Mn₄CaO₅ cluster: 2 H₂O → O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻; O₂ is a by-product, the O₂ we breathe |
| 4 | Lumen acidification | The H⁺ from water stay inside the thylakoid (lumen); this acid later drives ATP synthase and switches on photoprotection |
| 5 | Plastoquinone | Takes the electrons, plus H⁺ from the stroma, and carries them into the membrane as plastoquinol |
