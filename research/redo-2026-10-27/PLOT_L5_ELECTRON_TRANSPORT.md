# Chunk 5 · Electron transport routes, with ATP synthesis: concepts first, then the stories (27 Oct 2026, draft for the lecturer; nothing built)

Goes out to chunk 6: in bright light the chain overflows (photoprotection).

## Concepts students take home

| # | Concept | Take-home |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The chain on the membrane | PSII + LHCII in the grana; PSI and ATP synthase in the stroma lamellae; b₆f in both; plastoquinone and plastocyanin carry electrons between them |
| 2 | Two photosystems in series | One photon's push (~1.8 eV) cannot lift an electron from water to NADP⁺; PSII oxidises water, PSI reduces NADP⁺ (Z-scheme) |
| 3 | Cytochrome b₆f and PSI | b₆f passes electrons from plastoquinol to plastocyanin and adds H⁺ to the lumen; PSI → ferredoxin → NADPH in the stroma |
| 4 | ATP synthase, chemiosmosis | H⁺ flow back from the lumen through ATP synthase, turning its rotor: ATP |
| 5 | Routes and balance | Linear flow gives ≈ 2.6 ATP per 2 NADPH, the cycle needs 3 : 2; cyclic flow adds ATP; state transitions move LHCII to PSI |
