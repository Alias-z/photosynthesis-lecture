## Research done for the lecturer's request (Sage & Busch, refixation)
- **Busch, Sage, Cousins & Sage 2013** (Plant Cell Environ., full text):
  - chloroplasts cover 92.5 % (wheat) and 98.9 % (rice) of the mesophyll wall facing the air spaces;
  - 26 % (wheat) and 29 % (rice) of the photorespired CO₂ is refixed inside the cell;
  - net CO₂ uptake rises by 9 % and 11 %;
  - their Fig. 5 (chloroplast cover vs C*) is redrawn as C11 v4.
- **Busch 2020**, Fig. 2: the chloroplast barrier.
- **Khoshravesh et al. 2016** (CC BY): C₂ grasses have C* of 10.8–20 µmol mol⁻¹, against ≈ 50 in C₃.
- **Sage, Khoshravesh & Sage 2014**: the *Flaveria* series C₃ → proto-Kranz → C₂ → C₄.
- **Sage & Sage 2009** (rice anatomy): abstract only, because of the OUP bot check.
