**Currency (optional hooks; would need discussion before adoption):**
- **Scale of photosynthesis.** Global NPP is about 105 Pg C yr⁻¹, roughly half on land and half in the ocean ([Field et al. 1998](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.281.5374.237), A).
- **Land GPP is being revised upward but is disputed.** A carbonyl-sulfide tracer gives 157 ± 8.5 Pg C yr⁻¹ against the historical ~120 Pg C yr⁻¹ ([Lai et al. 2024, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08050-3), A). A 2026 Matters Arising challenges the tropical part of that estimate ([Tian et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10562-z), M). If used at all, present it as "debated".
- **Efficiency ceiling.** The theoretical maximum conversion of solar energy to biomass is 4.6% for C₃ and 6% for C₄ ([Zhu, Long & Ort 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.copbio.2008.02.004), A).

**Gap:** Water oxidation and O₂ release should appear in the opening sentence. A sketch of the redox logic, H₂O → electrons → NADPH → reduce CO₂ ([Johnson 2016](https://doi.org/10.1042/EBC20160016), F), would set up L08.

**Currency (2023–2026):**
- **Both edges of PAR are porous.** UV-A1 at 365–385 nm drives net assimilation weakly, sometimes negatively, depending on flavonoid screening ([Sun et al. 2025, PCE](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.15145), A). In tomato under artificial solar spectra, removing far-red lowered CO₂ uptake only at low light, while long-term far-red increased leaf area ([Lazzarin et al. 2025, Ann Bot](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcae104), A).
- **Far-red oxygenic photosynthesis.** Structural review: [Gisriel 2024, BBA](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbabio.2024.149032) (A). FaRLiP probably appeared early in cyanobacterial evolution, possibly in Palaeoproterozoic mats: [Antonaru et al. 2025, Curr Biol](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2025.04.038) (A).
- **Measured solar spectrum.** The slide is titled "The solar spectrum" but shows none. The ASTM G173-03 reference spectrum (T; [ANSI listing](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/astm/astmg173032020); NREL host unreachable from here) could be shown beside the prism.
- **The spectrum's "peak" depends on units.** It lies in the green per wavelength, in the near-IR per frequency, and shifts again in photon units ([Soffer & Lynch 1999](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.19170), A). This defuses the "why don't leaves absorb the peak?" misconception.

**Gaps:**
1. Photon-flux units (PPFD, µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹). Full sunlight is ≈2000 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ ([Croce & van Amerongen 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-013-9838-x), F).
2. The difference between absorption spectrum, action spectrum and quantum yield is only mentioned in passing (McCree). It is essential and links to L05 and L06.

**Gap (essential, short):** after absorption, a blue photon's extra energy is lost as heat within picoseconds. Photochemistry starts from the lowest excited state (Kasha's rule, [Kasha 1950](https://doi.org/10.1039/DF9500900014), M). In LHCII, excitation equilibrates within a few ps onto the lowest-energy Chls at ~680 nm ([van Amerongen & Croce 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-013-9824-3), F). Photosynthesis therefore counts photons, not joules, which is why PAR is defined in moles of photons. This one sentence ties L03, L04 and L05 together.

**Currency (2023–2026):**
- **Green light in crops.** A meta-analysis of 136 datasets in 17 crop species, all under controlled environments, found green light "similarly effective at increasing biomass as red and blue light". Its effects were species-dependent and it raised water-use efficiency ([Chen et al. 2024, J Exp Bot](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erae259), A).
- **Why algae use green.** Simulations combined with engineered cyanobacteria suggest Archaean oceans were green-lit by Fe(III), which favoured phycoerythrobilin antennas ([Matsuo et al. 2025, Nat Ecol Evol](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-025-02637-3), A). This is a modelling and engineering inference, not direct evidence.
- **Why green? Theories, as instructor reserve.** All are models; none is settled.
  - Noise-cancelling antenna: [Arp et al. 2020, Science](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba6630) (A).
  - Extended to other stars: [Duffy et al. 2023, MNRAS](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stad2823) (A) and [Chitnavis et al. 2024, Photosynth Res](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-024-01118-1) (A).
  - Cost-based counter-argument: [Marosvölgyi & van Gorkom 2010](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-009-9522-3) (A).

**Gaps:**
1. Carotenoids also protect, by quenching Chl triplets ([Hashimoto et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-39126-7_4), A). The context presents them only as light-capture extenders, and their protective role is the hook for L13.
2. Show an absorption spectrum next to an action spectrum, with the red drop.
3. Give one whole-leaf absorptance number: red/blue 80–95%, green 50–90%.

**Currency (2023–2026):**

- **Far-red in land plants.** A strongly red-shifted PSI in the shade plant *Fittonia* traces to Lhca3 sequence regions ([Li X et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50655-9), A). Mechanism of the red forms: Capaldi 2025 (table above).
- **Far-red chlorophylls.**
  - Chl f: 2-formyl-Chl a, in vitro absorption at 706 nm ([Chen et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1191127), A).
  - Chl d: [Miyashita et al. 1996](https://doi.org/10.1038/383402a0) (M).
  - FaRLiP: [Gan et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1256963) (A).
  - The cost of using low-energy photons: Chl d-PSII is more prone to photodamage ([Viola et al. 2022, eLife](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.79890), A).
  - Good BSc hook: moving or adding one formyl group (Chl b, d, f) retunes colour.
- **The quantum-coherence debate (instructor reserve).** The claim of wavelike transfer ([Engel et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05678)) was followed by evidence that electronic coherence decays in ~60 fs at ambient temperature ([Duan et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1702261114)). A consensus review concluded such coherences are "too short lived to have any functional significance" ([Cao et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aaz4888)). Full simulations have since been claimed to show long-lived excitonic coherence in FMO ([Lorenzoni et al. 2025, Sci Adv](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ady6751)); see also the review by [Jha et al. 2026, Chem Soc Rev](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5cs00948k). All of these are A and concern **bacterial** FMO or LH2, not plant LHCII. Teach it as an open question.
- **Single photons are enough.** Single-photon excitation drives energy transfer in LH2 ([Li Q et al. 2023, Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06121-5), A; purple bacterium).

**Gaps (essential):**
1. **Why antennas exist.** In full sun (2000 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹), the whole PSI-LHCI complex, ~170 Chl, absorbs one photon per ~600 µs ([Croce & van Amerongen 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-013-9838-x), F). That is roughly ten photons per second per chlorophyll, far below what a reaction centre can process.
2. **Timescale competition.** Charge separation takes <100 ps against a Chl excited-state lifetime of "several ns", giving φ > 0.95 (φ = 1 − τ/τ_Chl; [van Amerongen & Croce 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-013-9824-3), F). This bridges to L07 and L12.

**Too technical for this audience:** per-structure pigment inventories (45/12/13; they vary between structures), "thermally assisted uphill transfer" as a term, and Lhca numbering. Keep the contrast "LHCII b-rich (a:b ≈ 1.3) vs LHCI a-rich (≈ 3.7) with red forms". Put the counts in the Context only.
