**Currency 2023–2026:**
- [Greife et al. 2023, Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06008-5) (Abs) used microsecond IR spectroscopy to characterise the S₄ state, postulated 50 years earlier, as an oxygen-radical state. A proton vacancy forms first; the following step, which forms the radical by moving one electron and several protons, is the slowest. This is the companion to Bhowmick 2023.
- [Li et al. 2024, Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06987-5) (Abs) watched a water molecule bind near Ca/D1-E189 and become O6.
- The O6 interpretation is publicly contested (Wang 2024 against Li et al. 2026).
- The draft's rule is right: teach the four-step clock and the Mn₄CaO₅ catalyst firmly, and keep atomistic O–O mechanisms out.

**BSc hook:** "A state predicted in 1970 was first characterised in 2023."

**Currency 2023–2026 (cyclic flow):**
- Two 2026 papers read the same mutant in opposite ways. Degen 2026 holds that PGR5 is needed for CEF/proton influx, not as an ATP-synthase brake, and that CEF is not needed for ATP. Nikkanen 2026 holds that PGR5 is not needed for CEF but brakes ATP synthase.
- Nakamura 2026 shows that in C₄ plants both pathways matter for ATP and PSI protection.
- Emrich-Mills 2025 (*Chlamydomonas*) shows that tethering FNR to PSI shifts flux toward CEF.
- Johnson 2025 Box 1 notes that NDH is only about 1% of PSI in Arabidopsis but abundant in C₄ bundle sheath. The PSI–NDH supercomplex structure has been solved ([Shen 2022 Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04277-6) Meta).
- **Teach:** CEF exists and protects PSI; how the PGR5 route works is unknown; and whether CEF's main job is ATP or ΔpH is an open 2026 debate.
- **Hook:** Johnson's 2025 review restates the CEF-for-ATP view, and his own group's 2026 paper challenges it.

## Gaps (priority order)

1. **ATP:NADPH mismatch is not stated where cyclic flow is taught (L10/L11).** Linear flow gives about 1.29 against 1.5 for the CBB cycle (about 1.56 with photorespiration); the numbers appear only in C04. Give the three balancing routes (CEF, water–water/FLV, malate-valve export) and show that the gap depends on H⁺/ATP. This is the core logic students need later. Sources: [Johnson 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-025-00847-y) Box 1; [Kramer & Evans 2011](https://doi.org/10.1104/pp.110.166652); [Degen 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-026-02374-w).
2. **CEF → ΔpH → photosynthetic control at b₆f + NPQ → PSI protection.** Add that PSI has no repair cycle and that pgr5 fails in fluctuating light. This is the robust, consensus phenotype, and the 2026 debate is about whether it is CEF's main job. Sources: [Suorsa 2012](https://doi.org/10.1105/tpc.112.097162), [Munekage 2002](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0092-8674(02)00867-X), Johnson 2025.
3. **Thylakoid architecture and lateral heterogeneity are absent ("grana" has zero hits in the drafts).** PSII–LHCII sit in the grana; PSI, ATP synthase and PSI–NDH in the stroma lamellae; b₆f in both. That separation is why mobile PQ and PC are needed. In situ cryo-ET of spinach chloroplasts shows strict PSII/PSI segregation at the grana boundary, with no evidence for a mixed grana-margin zone, which supports a two-domain model ([Wietrzynski et al. 2025 eLife](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.105496), Abs; Johnson 2025 Fig. 4 FT). It fits on the L07 membrane map.
4. **Realistic pmf numbers.** Lumen pH is about 5.7–7.5 in vivo, not 4–5, and the Δψ share varies (0.1–0.7). Ion channels tune the split. Sources: [Takizawa 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbabio.2007.07.006), [Li 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-021-00947-5).
5. **Why water can be oxidised:** P680⁺ is about +1.2 V against roughly +0.82–0.87 V for O₂/H₂O (Johnson 2025). One number.
6. **ATP synthase dark inactivation by redox switch** (why it does not hydrolyse ATP at night). Source: [Hahn 2018](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat4318).
7. **State transitions and D1 repair** are only reserve lines in L13. Johnson 2025 FT gives STN7 sensing PQH₂ at b₆f and PSII damage roughly every 30 minutes in high light. Give one foreshadowing sentence at the PQ pool in L09/L11.
8. **Measured quantum requirement** of about 9.4 absorbed photons per O₂ against the ideal 8 ([Björkman & Demmig 1987](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00402983)).

## Story candidates ("then → now")

1. **Cyclic photophosphorylation, 1954 → 2026 debate (top pick).**
   - Then: Arnon, Allen & Whatley showed light-driven ATP formation by isolated chloroplasts ([Nature 1954](https://doi.org/10.1038/174394a0); [JACS 1954](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01653a025)). The route was later linked to ferredoxin ([Tagawa et al. 1963](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.49.4.567), Meta). The pgr5 and double mutants then made CEF look essential ([Munekage 2002](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0092-8674(02)00867-X), [2004](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02598)).
   - Now: [Degen 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-026-02374-w) reports ATP unchanged, [Nikkanen 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbabio.2026.149596) a PGR5 brake on ATP synthase, and [Nakamura 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag146) that C₄ plants need both pathways.
   - Message: a 70-year-old pathway whose purpose is being re-argued this year.
2. **Flash oscillations → XFEL movies (top pick).**
   - Then: [Joliot et al. 1969](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-1097.1969.tb05696.x) found O₂ peaking at flash 3 and every 4 flashes after; [Kok et al. 1970](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-1097.1970.tb06017.x) explained it with the S-state clock.
   - Now: XFEL snapshots of all S-states ([Kern 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0681-2)) and the S₃→S₀ step ([Bhowmick 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06038-z)); S₄ characterised by IR ([Greife 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06008-5)); an open dispute ([Wang 2024](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2059798324011392) against [Li et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2059798326003621)).
   - A damped period-four plot animates well.
3. **Where does the O₂ come from?**
   - Then: van Niel's comparative hypothesis ([1932](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00454965), Meta), then [Ruben 1941](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01848a512), then mass-spectrometric flash yields ([Radmer & Ollinger 1980](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(80)80022-6); [Messinger 1995](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.92.8.3209)).
   - Now: [Li 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06987-5) watches a substrate water entering via Ca.
   - Caveat: the 1941 limitation is unverified from primary text.
4. **Chemiosmosis.**
   - Then: [Mitchell 1961](https://doi.org/10.1038/191144a0) (controversial; Nobel 1978), then [Jagendorf & Uribe 1966](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.55.1.170), then rotation seen directly ([Noji 1997](https://doi.org/10.1038/386299a0)).
   - Now: the c₁₄ cryo-EM structure ([Hahn 2018](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat4318)) and the open H⁺/ATP puzzle (4.67 against 3.9; [Petersen 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1202799109)).
5. **The quantum-yield war.**
   - Then: Warburg claimed about 4 photons per O₂; Emerson and colleagues measured 8–12 ([Hill & Govindjee 2014 review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11120-014-0014-8), Meta).
   - Now: about 9.4 absorbed photons per O₂ across 37 C₃ species ([Björkman & Demmig 1987](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00402983)), against the Z-scheme minimum of 8. Note that the historical sources are Govindjee-authored.
6. **Two lights, two places.**
   - Then: [Emerson 1957](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.43.1.133), [Hill & Bendall 1960](https://doi.org/10.1038/186136a0) and [Duysens et al. 1961](https://doi.org/10.1038/190510a0).
   - Now: in situ cryo-ET shows PSII and PSI physically segregated yet coupled by mobile carriers ([Wietrzynski 2025](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.105496)).
