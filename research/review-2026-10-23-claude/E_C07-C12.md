# Review E: C07–C12 and the FvCB block of ENERGY_ACCOUNTING

Reviewer: Claude (independent check), 2026-10-23. Scope: `CONTEXT_REVIEW.md` sections C07–C12 (the matching `drafts.json` strings are identical; I checked every string longer than 60 characters), plus `ENERGY_ACCOUNTING.md`, "Carbon demand" (C4 note) and "FvCB bookkeeping for the instructor".

**Method.** I checked every cited DOI against Crossref metadata, which gives authors, year, journal, volume and pages and lists any correction or retraction notice. Abstracts came from Europe PMC, full texts from PMC where noted. Crossref lists no correction or retraction notice for any of the 14 cited works.

**Evidence levels.**
- **A**: I read the primary abstract or full text, and it supports the claim with the right scope.
- **B**: support comes from metadata plus a review or secondary account, or the claim is standard textbook stoichiometry.
- **C**: not verified.

**Verdicts.** CONFIRMED / WRONG / OUTDATED / OVERSTATED / UNSUPPORTED / UNVERIFIED.

**Totals:** 61 claims checked, plus 3 suggested additions. 0 WRONG, 1 OUTDATED, 2 OVERSTATED, 1 UNSUPPORTED as cited, plus 2 attribution or scope fixes. All 14 cited papers exist, and their metadata matches the draft.

## Currency notes (2023–2026)

**C4 rice and C4 engineering.** No functional C4 rice has been reported. I found no peer-reviewed report up to October 2026.
- **Funding.** The C4 Rice Project (Oxford-led; Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation funding) received an extension in July 2024 that runs to May 2026 ([c4rice.com, 20 Jul 2024](https://c4rice.com/2024/07/20/phase-iv-extension-funding-approved/)).
- **Biochemistry.**
  - Five maize C4 enzymes on one construct raised PEPC flux 10-fold, which is still only about 2 % of maize photosynthetic flux. Flux from malate to pyruvate to PEP stayed low ([Ermakova et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/pbi.13487)).
  - An earlier four-enzyme line labelled malate but showed no flux into the CBB cycle ([Lin et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2020.564463)).
- **Anatomy.**
  - Maize GLK genes induced proto-Kranz-like organelle development in rice ([Wang et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2017.09.040)).
  - Raising SHR and auxin together gave a C4-like vein pattern ([Dong et al. 2023, Sci Bull](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scib.2023.10.005)).
- **Regulatory parts.**
  - C4 bundle-sheath genes acquired an ancestral DOF cis-code that already exists in C3 rice ([Swift et al. 2024, Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08204-3)).
  - A short rice cis-regulatory module (CRM) drives BS-specific expression and can be tuned up to 220-fold ([Hua et al. 2025, Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62087-0)).
- **Why rice is hard.** Rice belongs to the BEP clade, which lacks the anatomical enablers for C4 ([Christin 2013](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1216777110)).

**C2 engineering.** Lundgren proposed it as a medium-term route ([2020 Tansley insight](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.16494)). Regulatory parts now exist ([Dickinson 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-025-00461-1); [Hua 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62087-0)). I found no peer-reviewed engineered C2 crop.

**CAM engineering.**
- Reviews: [Yang et al. 2024, CSH Perspect](https://doi.org/10.1101/cshperspect.a041674); [Lim et al. 2025, SynCAM, Phil Trans B](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0249).
- [Wu et al. 2025, Adv Sci](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202500418) report a "CAM-like" shuttle in rice. Night malate went to the TCA cycle, and yield rose about 20 % over two field years, but WUE and drought tolerance did **not** improve.
- This is a single study and not CAM in the functional sense. Do not present it as "CAM rice".

**C4 and CAM evolution.**
- Flaveria chromosome-scale genomes point to transcriptional regulation as the main driver of C4 evolution ([Lyu et al. 2025, Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56901-y)).
- Blepharis: [Stata 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.70426).
- CAM: at least 66 independent origins, in about 7 % of vascular plant species ([Gilman et al. 2023, Ann Bot](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcad135)).
- C4: 61 lineages and about 8,100 species ([Sage 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erw156)).

**Mesophyll conductance and Ci.**
- Even Ci is a derived value. It assumes saturated intercellular air, but substomatal relative humidity can fall to about 80 % at large VPD ([Wong et al. 2022, Nat Plants](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-022-01202-1); [Márquez et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-021-00861-w); [Márquez et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.18784)).
- Reviews of how complex gm is: [Evans 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.16968); [Busch et al. 2024 guide](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.14815).
- Method artefacts are still debated: gm from curve fitting differs from gm by the variable-J method ([Wang et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.15006)).
- Many land-surface models omit gm ([Knauer et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1111/tpj.14587)).
- Instructor-level only.

**Respiration in the light (R_L).** Its source is unresolved: [Sharkey & Xu 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.70216) argue for the OPPP G6P shunt, while [Abadie et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.14871) find multiple sources.

**FvCB extensions.**
- Use min-W, not min-A, and include the TPU CO₂ threshold ([Lochocki & McGrath 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/insilicoplants/diaf014)).
- TPU is argued to be unnecessary in terrestrial biosphere models ([Rogers et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.17092)).
- An FvCB term for nitrogen assimilation through photorespiration: [Busch, Sage & Farquhar 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-017-0065-x).
- C4 model update with cyclic electron flow and 4 H⁺/ATP: [von Caemmerer 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erab266).

## Gaps (essential for a BSc with no background)

1. **Stomata as the gate for CO₂ supply, and the water cost.** C11 has only "Ca → Ci". Before Ci/Cc, add:
   - A = gs(Ca − Ci);
   - stomata trade CO₂ gain against water loss;
   - this trade-off is the reason C4 and CAM matter.

   Sources: [Hetherington & Woodward 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01843); [Lawson & Vialet-Chabrand 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.15330); [Busch 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.14815).
2. **Why C4 matters ecologically and agriculturally.** None of this is in C07–C12:
   - more than 60 independent origins (61 lineages; [Sage 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erw156));
   - about 3 % of species but about 23 % of terrestrial gross primary production ([Still et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1029/2001GB001807));
   - maize, sorghum and sugarcane; sugarcane alone was more than 2 Gt of the 9.9 Gt of primary crops in 2023 ([FAO](https://www.fao.org/statistics/highlights-archive/highlights-detail/agricultural-production-statistics-2010-2023/en); [von Caemmerer 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erab266));
   - dominance of warm, open grasslands ([Edwards et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1177216));
   - origins linked to the Oligocene CO₂ decline ([Christin 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2007.11.058));
   - quantum-yield crossover with temperature ([Ehleringer & Björkman 1977](https://doi.org/10.1104/pp.59.1.86); [Ehleringer et al. 1997](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004420050311));
   - higher light-, nitrogen- and water-use efficiency ([Wang 2014](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/eru058)).
3. **Rising CO₂ and C3 versus C4.**
   - Today's C3 leaf sits on the Rubisco-limited, CO₂-responsive part of the A–Ci curve; C4 is near saturation.
   - In FACE experiments C4 crops gain no yield except under drought, while C3 crops gain about 18 % ([Ainsworth & Long 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8137.2004.01224.x); [2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.15375)).
   - The 20-year reversal in grassland ([Reich et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aas9313)) is a good counter-example.
   - This links FvCB (C12) with C4 (C09) at no extra cost.
4. **Kranz anatomy is never defined.** C07 uses "proto-Kranz" without first explaining Kranz: the wreath of bundle sheath around closely spaced veins. Single-cell C4 can be a one-line footnote.
5. **CAM needs its purpose and scale.** Give one line on water saving and on "≥66 origins, ~7 % of species" ([Gilman 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcad135)). The C4 + CAM same-cell result ([Moreno-Villena 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abn2349)) makes a better qualifier than the current Portulaca sentence.
6. **One line of engineering status** would meet the "state of the art" brief: no functional C4 rice yet; partial biochemistry and partial anatomy achieved (see Currency).

**Too technical for this audience.**
- **Full FvCB equations in student-facing text.** The C12 paragraph "Keep the meaning of the quantities clear" gives Aj with Γ*. Move it, with Wc and Kc/Ko, to a backup slide or the instructor notes.
- **What students should get instead.** One A–Ci schematic: a Rubisco-limited region at low CO₂, a regeneration (light/electron)-limited plateau, and an optional dashed TPU cap. Name the two knobs, Vcmax and Jmax, and use the authors' own "see-saw" image.
- **A C4 overlay.** Add a C4 curve that saturates at low Ci. It explains C4 and rising CO₂ in one picture.
- **Keep for instructors only:**
  - min-A versus min-W;
  - the origin of R_L in the G6P shunt;
  - methods for estimating gm.
- **Keep A = Vc − 0.5Vo − R_L.** It is accessible bookkeeping.
