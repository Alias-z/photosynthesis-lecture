- WRONG: false.
- OUT: outdated, or called "recent" when it is not.

- WORD: wording or framing.

### (b) Sugar export: closing the "bread" promise

**C04b, "Sucrose by day, starch for the night" (2 min)**
- **Export.** Triose-P leaves on the TPT in exchange for Pi ([Flügge 1999](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.arplant.50.1.27)). Sucrose synthesis returns the Pi. If it lags, ATP synthesis slows: this is TPU (C12).
- **Starch.** Starch is made in the stroma. At night it is exported as maltose ([Niittylä 2004](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1091811)), at a clock-set rate that lasts until dawn ([Graf 2010](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0914299107); [Smith & Zeeman 2020](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-arplant-050718-100241)).
- **Phloem.** SWEET efflux, then SUT loading ([Chen 2012](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1213351); [Braun 2022](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-arplant-070721-083240)). Pressure flow moves the sucrose ([Knoblauch 2016](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.15341)).

**C13, "From leaf to loaf" (1 min)**
- Do not draw "leaf starch → grain starch". Wheat stores day carbon as sucrose and stem fructans ([Schnyder 1993](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8137.1993.tb03731.x)).
- The ear feeds grain filling more than the flag-leaf blade, which gives 3–18 % ([Sanchez-Bragado 2014](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/eru298)).
- The endosperm turns sucrose into ADP-glucose (by a cytosolic AGPase in grasses) and then starch ([Beckles 2001](https://doi.org/10.1104/pp.125.2.818); [Emes 2003](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erg089)).
- Wheat gives ~20 % of dietary calories ([Shiferaw 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12571-013-0263-y); verify before use).
