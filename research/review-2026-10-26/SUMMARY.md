## 2. What is missing

**Where things sit (asked by the lecturer).** No slide text names the compartments. The figures exist but are unpicked (L01 chloroplast, 6E, 6J) or unlabelled (L07 envelope).

| Item | One line | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Compartments | Envelope (two membranes) → stroma (Calvin–Benson cycle; NADPH and ATP made here) → thylakoid membrane (all complexes) → lumen (water oxidation, H⁺, PC) | Kirchhoff 2019 |
| Pigments sit in proteins | Every chlorophyll and carotenoid is held by a protein in the thylakoid membrane, ~1 nm apart; LHCII with PSII in the grana, LHCI with PSI in unstacked membrane | Croce & van Amerongen 2020 |
| Who sits where, and why | PSII in stacked grana. PSI and ATP synthase only in unstacked membrane: their stromal heads (ATP synthase ~12 nm) do not fit the 3.2 nm gap between stacked discs. b₆f sits in both | Wietrzynski 2025 |
| Mobile carriers | PQ in the membrane, PC in the lumen, Fd in the stroma; FNR makes NADPH; b₆f (Q cycle) is the slow step | Malone 2019 |
| How energy hops | Without emitting light, to a neighbour ~1 nm away (Förster); it reaches the reaction centre in 50 ps (PSI) to 0.3 ns (PSII) | Förster 1948; Wientjes 2013 |
| Origin | Chloroplasts are former cyanobacteria; fossil cells had thylakoids 1.75 billion years ago | Demoulin 2024 |

**Other electron routes (asked by the lecturer).** Slide 11 draws "to O₂" but never names a pathway.

| Route | What it does | Size in a C₃ leaf | Teach? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyclic flow: PGR5 (main) and NDH | Fd → PQ → b₆f → PSI: pumps H⁺, makes no NADPH | Below linear flow, except in the first seconds; NDH carries 84 % in C₄ bundle-sheath cells | Yes (on the slide) |
| Water–water cycle (Mehler–Asada) | PSI reduces O₂ → superoxide → H₂O₂ → water; pumps H⁺, no NADPH, no net O₂ | Under 10 % in flowering plants | Yes: name it |
| Flavodiiron proteins (FLV) | Reduce O₂ straight to water, without ROS, in the first seconds of strong light | Large in cyanobacteria, algae, mosses and conifers; **lost in flowering plants** | Yes: as an evolution story |
| Malate valve | Exports spare NADPH as malate | Unknown; knockouts look normal | One arrow (already there) |
| Chlororespiration (PTOX) | PQ → O₂ in darkness | Negligible in light | Instructor notes |
| State transitions (STN7) | Moves LHCII between the photosystems: an antenna process, not an electron route | Modest; matters in fluctuating light | Slide 13 or 6, optional |

Not routes, but students confuse them: **photorespiration**, the largest O₂ sink in C₃ leaves, belongs to lecture 2. Nitrite reduction also uses Fd.

## 3. Where newer work overturns the textbook

| Textbook | Now | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A mixed "grana margin" between grana and stroma lamellae | A sharp border; no mixing zone in intact chloroplasts | Wietrzynski 2025 |
| PSII in crystalline arrays (EM images) | Non-crystalline in place; the arrays are extraction artefacts | Wietrzynski 2025 |
| Purified PSII–LHCII (C₂S₂M₂) | In rice chloroplasts PSII carries four more LHCII trimers, lost on purification | Li 2026 |
| LHCII feeds PSII and moves to PSI only in "state 2" | LHCII feeds both photosystems most of the time | Wientjes 2013 |
| PAR = 400–700 nm | Far-red adds 10–25 % of leaf photosynthesis in sunlight | Zhen 2022 |
| P680 is a "special pair" that gives the first electron | The electron leaves a neighbouring chlorophyll (ChlD1) first; the + ends on P680 | Holzwarth 2006 |
| Lumen pH 4–5 | About 6 (± 0.5) | Takizawa 2007 |
| pmf = ΔpH + Δψ in comparable parts | Mostly ΔpH in steady light (debated) | Wilson 2021 |
| C₃ leaves need cyclic flow for ATP | Disputed: ATP stays normal with 50–75 % of the pmf | Degen 2026 |
| PGR5 drives cyclic flow | It may instead brake ATP synthase | Nikkanen 2026 |
| The Mehler reaction is a major outlet | Small in flowering plants; algae use FLV instead | Badger 2000; Ilík 2017 |
| "Dark reactions" | The cycle is light-regulated; the term comes from the 1905 "Blackman reaction" | Emerson & Arnold 1932 |
| Faster NPQ relaxation raises yield | Positive results come from one consortium; independent tests are null or negative | Zheng 2026; Singh 2026 |
