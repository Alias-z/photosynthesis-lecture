## 3. Where newer work refines the textbook

| Textbook | Now | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The CBB cycle is a closed loop | Sugar carbon re-enters it, ≈ 5 % of net fixation | Xu 2022 |
| Leaves store starch for the night | True for Arabidopsis-type leaves; wheat stores sucrose, and its stems fructans | Schnyder 1993 |
| The flag leaf feeds the grain | The ear contributes as much as the shoot or more | Sanchez-Bragado 2014, 2016 |
| TPU is the third limit on photosynthesis | It rarely limits at today's CO₂; global models drop it | Gregory 2021; McClain 2023 |
| The clock sets night-time starch use | The pacing survives major clock disruption; the mechanism is open | Moraes 2022 |
| Rubisco is near-optimal | Lab variants are faster without losing specificity | McDonald 2026; Chaudhari 2026; Prywes 2025 |
| Photorespiration is waste | Essential, and it feeds N, serine and one-carbon metabolism | Busch 2018; Fu 2023 |
| Photorespiratory bypasses give +40 % | +11–24 % in small trials; larger gains in heat | South 2019; Cavanagh 2022; 2026 review |
| C₂ is a step on the way to C₄ | Often a stable state; it arose ≥ 5 times in Brassicaceae from a switch already present in C₃ plants | Dickinson 2025; Triesch 2024 |
| CO₂ fertilisation raises yields by about a third | ≈ 18 % in C₃ crops without stress; ≈ 10 % with low N or heat; less Zn, Fe and protein | Ainsworth & Long 2021 |

## 6. The order

**Proposal:** [ORDER_LECTURE2.md](../figure-briefs/ORDER_LECTURE2.md).
- **Variant 1 (recommended; all four reviewers reached it independently):**
  - The cycle (C1–C4).
  - Sugar export (C4b; the phloem moves to C13).
  - Rubisco and O₂ (C5), photorespiration (C6).
  - **Whole leaf moved up:** gas exchange, Γ and water (C11), then FvCB on one C₃ curve (C12).
  - C₂ (C7 + C8 merged, anatomy first), C₄ (C9), CAM (C10).
  - **Rising CO₂: C₃ vs C₄** (from C12).
  - Leaf to loaf (C13).
- **Why:** the current order uses stomata, Γ and leaf anatomy before teaching them, and puts the C₃ model (FvCB) after C₄ and CAM.
- **The cost:** about 6 minutes between photorespiration's glycine step and the C₂ slide that moves it; one recall line bridges them.
- **Variants 2 and 3** make smaller changes.
- **Time:** 43 minutes in all three.
