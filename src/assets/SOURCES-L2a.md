`photos/c1-taylor.jpg`: Taylor SJ, Long SP (2017) Slow induction of photosynthesis on shade to sun transitions in wheat may cost at least 21% of productivity. *Phil Trans R Soc B* 372: 20160543, https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0543, **Fig. 1b**.
