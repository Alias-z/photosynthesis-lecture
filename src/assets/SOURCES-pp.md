# Slides 11–13, photoprotection and chlorophyll fluorescence (chunk pp, 28 Oct 2026)

Modules `src/pilot/figs/pp11f.ts`, `pp12f.ts`, `pp13f.ts` (figures) and `pp11.ts`, `pp12.ts`, `pp13.ts` (text); shared kit `pp-kit.ts`, styles `pp.css`. No new image files: the figures reuse the restyled crops of block L1c (`photos/12-*`, `13-*`, `14-*`, see `SOURCES-L1c.md`) and three images of the reference board `refs/L6-photoprotection/` (listed below). Paper figures keep their white ground (multiplied into the page) and carry a grey credit line. White (or ground-coloured) rectangles and discs cover the figures' own small labels, panel letters and numbered keys, which are set again in the deck fonts at the same positions. No data are masked or changed. Values "read off the figure" were digitised by eye from the image at the stated magnification; they are drawn as points and are never printed as numbers.
